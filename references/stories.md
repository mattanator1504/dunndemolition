### The hydraulic thumb

An excavator bucket on its own can push and scoop, but it can't grip. A hydraulic thumb is a powered claw that closes against the bucket, so the operator can pinch a beam, pull a wall section exactly where they want it to fall, and pick debris apart by material as they go. That's why the demolition stays controlled, and why concrete, metal and wood can be sorted onsite instead of in a landfill.

**Use when:** how demolition works, safety, no explosives, recycling, choosing a contractor.

### From driveway to 21A

Clean concrete from a demolished slab, driveway or foundation comes back to the yard and gets crushed and screened by size. 1½" minus becomes crusher run 21A, 2–3" pieces become #3 construction entrance stone, and big chunks become concrete rip rap. The finished stone meets or exceeds VDOT specifications and comes with an engineered proctor, then goes back out under new driveways, pads and roads.

**Use when:** recycling, buying stone, 21A versus #57, sustainability, "where does the debris go?"

### Haul as you go

Debris is loaded into steel dump trailers and dump trucks continuously while the building comes down, instead of sitting in a pile until the last day. The site stays cleaner and safer, neighbors see less mess, and the next trade can start sooner.

**Use when:** the demolition process, what to expect on demo day, residential demolition with neighbors close by.

### The paperwork before the noise

Before a whole structure comes down, there's usually a demolition permit to pull and utilities (gas, electric, water, sewer) to disconnect, each with its own municipal steps. Dunn's office handles or helps with this. On many jobs it takes longer than the demolition itself, which is a big part of why lead time is typically 7–14 days.

**Use when:** permits, timelines, "how long does demolition take?", first-time homeowners.

### Making a pool disappear

Removing a pool means taking out the vinyl liner or breaking up the concrete shell, removing the surrounding apron or deck, then backfilling and grading the area so it can be used again.

**Use when:** pool removal, backyard projects, selling a house with a pool.

### Tanks: pump, then pull

An above- or below-ground oil tank, or a septic tank, isn't just dug up. Any remaining product gets pumped out and disposed of properly first, then the empty tank is removed.

**Use when:** oil tank removal, septic removal, buying or selling an older home, site prep.
